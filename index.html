Understanding Appliance Energy Consumption

Welcome to the Website, a small demonstration site exploring how much energy household appliances use in the Australian market. Rising electricity costs and environmental awareness make it more important than ever to understand how much power our appliances actually draw.

This site focuses on televisions which is one of the most common appliances in Australian homes and looks at how screen size, technology, and brand affect energy use. Use the navigation menu above to explore the data, or learn more about this project on the About Us page.
