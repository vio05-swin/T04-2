Television Energy Consumption

Larger screens and older display technologies (like plasma) tend to use more energy, while modern LED and OLED televisions are generally more efficient. Standby power also adds up switching TVs off fully, rather than leaving them on standby, can reduce unnecessary electricity use.

  • Check the energy rating label and estimated annual kWh before buying.
  • Use "Eco" or power-saving picture modes where available.
  • Turn off always-on features like voice assistances if unused.
